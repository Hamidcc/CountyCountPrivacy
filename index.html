❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is CountyCount completely free?
Yes. Every single feature is 100% free. No premium tiers, no paywalls, no hidden costs.
Do I need to vote to use features?
Voting is optional and only unlocks minor cosmetic easter eggs. All core features work without voting.
How do I add CountyCount to my server?
Click Invite, authorize the bot, then run /setup or /config channel #your-channel. Setup typically completes in under 30 seconds.
What permissions are required?
Read Messages, Send Messages, Add Reactions, Manage Messages (for cleanup), and optionally Manage Channels for voice status features.
Can I use CountyCount in multiple channels?
Currently one counting channel per server. Multi-channel support is planned on the roadmap.
Does the bot store data?
Only counting statistics, leaderboards and minimal logs required for integrity. Opt-out is available with /snipe.
How do I delete my data?
Use /snipe @yourself to remove your statistics from a server. Inactive servers may be pruned after 90+ days.
What is Force Mode & Strict Force Mode?
Force Mode keeps the counting channel clean by deleting non-count messages. Strict Force Mode deletes wrong numbers instantly (no error messages).
Can I customize reactions, colors and templates?
Yes. Use /config emoji, /config color #HEX and /config templates (JSON) to customize behavior and messages.
What counting modes are available?
Decimal, Binary, Hexadecimal, UTF-8 characters and Greek letters. Additional modes (Roman, scientific) are planned.
What happens if the bot goes offline?
Counts are saved periodically (every ~10s). When the bot returns, counting resumes from the last saved number.
How does the global leaderboard work?
Global leaderboard aggregates counts across all servers. Use /globalleaderboard to view top counters worldwide.
